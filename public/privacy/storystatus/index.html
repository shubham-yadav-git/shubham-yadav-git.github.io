1. Introduction

Welcome to StoryStatus: Status Maker (“StoryStatus,” “we,” “our,” or “the app”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use StoryStatus to browse templates, create status images, customize text and backgrounds, save images, and share your creations.

2. Information We Collect

StoryStatus is designed to let you create and share status images without requiring an account.

Depending on the features and third-party services enabled in the app, the following information may be processed:

Information you provide

  • Text, quotes, Shayari, names, and messages you enter into the editor.
  • Photos or images you select, if the app supports importing personal images.
  • Preferences such as favorite templates and editing choices.

Information stored on your device

The app may store templates, preferences, favorites, and exported images locally on your device to provide its functionality.

Content created in the editor is not intentionally transmitted to our servers unless a feature you use explicitly requires it.

Automatically collected information

If analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services are integrated into the app, those services may collect information such as device information, app interactions, diagnostic logs, advertising identifiers, and approximate usage information, subject to their respective privacy policies and your available settings.

We will update this policy if the app's data practices change.

3. Photos, Media, and Storage

StoryStatus may allow you to create, save, and share images.

  • Images created in the app may be saved to your device's gallery or an appropriate media location.
  • The app may access photos or images you explicitly select if image-import functionality is provided.
  • The app should request only the permissions necessary for the features you use.
  • Images you save or share are under your control once exported, subject to the destination app's policies and your sharing choices.

StoryStatus does not need access to your entire photo library merely to create status images from built-in templates.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by the app may be used to:

  • Provide template browsing and image-editing functionality.
  • Save your preferences and favorite templates.
  • Export images in a format suitable for sharing.
  • Improve app stability, performance, and usability.
  • Diagnose crashes and technical problems if crash-reporting services are enabled.
  • Display and measure advertisements if advertising services are enabled.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing Your Information

StoryStatus does not intentionally share your personal content with us or other parties unless required for a feature you choose or a service integrated into the app.

You may choose to share an exported image using third-party applications, such as messaging or social media apps. Once you share content, the receiving service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the service.

The app may use third-party services to support functionality, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.

If integrated, these services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Examples include:

These services are examples, not a statement that every service is currently integrated into StoryStatus.

Information stored locally by StoryStatus generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or remove the relevant files.

If you have a privacy-related question or request, contact us using the details below.
